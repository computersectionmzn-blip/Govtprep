Refund Policy

Last updated: May 2026

At GovtPrep, we strive to provide the best learning experience. Our refund policy is as follows:

Free Access

All current courses and practice materials are offered free of charge. No refunds are applicable for free services.

Paid Services

If we introduce paid services in the future, the following will apply:

  • Full refund within 7 days of purchase if no more than 20% of the course has been accessed
  • Pro-rata refund for cancellations within 30 days
  • No refund after 30 days of purchase

Technical Issues

If you experience persistent technical issues preventing access, contact support for resolution or refund consideration.

How to Request

Email us at support@govtprep.in with your registered email and order details. We process requests within 5–7 business days.
