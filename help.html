Help Center

Find answers to common questions about GovtPrep.

How do I create an account?

Click "Sign In" on the homepage, then select "Create account". Fill in your name, email, and password to register.

How do I enroll in a course?

Browse courses from the Courses page and click "Enroll Free" on any course card to start learning.

How do I take a mock test?

Go to the Mock Tests section from your dashboard. Select an available test and click "Start Test".

How is my performance tracked?

Each test you take generates detailed analytics. Visit the Performance page to see your accuracy, rank, and subject-wise breakdown.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Click "Forgot Password?" on the login page and enter your email to receive reset instructions.

Is GovtPrep free?

We offer free access to courses, practice questions, and mock tests. Premium features may be added in the future.
